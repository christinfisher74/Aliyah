WHAT'S ON YOUR PLATE, AND WHY
Protein
Repairs and builds the muscle you're training for. Keeps you full longer. Every hormone and enzyme in your body needs it to work right — skimp on it and your body starts pulling it from muscle just to keep the lights on.
Carbs
Your brain and your muscles run on carbs first. Cut them too low and you feel it — foggy, tired, short-tempered. They're what lets you push through a hard set and still think clearly in clinicals.
Fats
Builds your hormones, including the ones that regulate your cycle, your mood, your metabolism. Helps you absorb vitamins A, D, E, K. Go too low and it shows up in your energy and mood before anywhere else.
Fiber
Keeps digestion moving and blood sugar steady, so you're not crashing two hours after you eat. Most people don't get enough and just feel "off" without knowing why.
Build your plate
No counting — just this. Half veggies and fiber, a quarter protein, a quarter carbs, a thumb of fat added in.

Front-load your day
If you've got a set amount to get in, eat more of it early — not late. Front-load breakfast and lunch instead of saving most of it for dinner. It's a lot easier to catch up on carbs and fat later than to cram everything in before an overnight shift, and it kills that 8pm "starving" feeling that leads to eating past full.
